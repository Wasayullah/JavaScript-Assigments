## 🧭 Philosophy

> **Learn it. Understand it. Build it yourself.**

I know my projects won't always look as polished as ones built with heavy AI generation — and that's fine by me. I'd rather ship something I fully understand than something that just looks impressive. Every repo here reflects what I can actually explain, debug, and extend on my own.
